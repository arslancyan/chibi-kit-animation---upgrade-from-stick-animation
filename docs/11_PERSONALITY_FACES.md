# Personality face library (reference rules)

A reference sheet of 20 default facial identities (quiet, friendly, cheerful, smug, sleepy, grumpy, serious, shy, sad, cool with sunglasses, nerdy with glasses, mischievous, and others). These are a character's default face, not a temporary emotion.

## Rules
- Keep the rounded-square head shape, proportions, skin colour, outline thickness and shading identical to the master base body.
- Differences come only from eyes, eyebrows and mouth (plus small items such as glasses, sunglasses, blush, star eyes).
- No hairstyles unless requested. Hair, clothes, accessories and colours are separate layers.
- Do not mix identities, redesign the character, or change body or head geometry.

## Workflow for a new character
1. Pick a personality from the sheet.
2. Reproduce that face on the master head.
3. Add hair, clothes, accessories as separate layers.
4. Build the 8 expressions from that identity so the personality stays recognisable.

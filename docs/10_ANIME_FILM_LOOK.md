# Anime-film HD stylized look (portrait 9:16, 1080x1920)

- World: Blender toon/cel shading with soft painterly textures, detailed sets, layered depth.
- Sky and light: anime-film skies and clouds, golden-hour or blue-night palettes held per scene, volumetric god rays, bloom.
- Camera: slow cinematic dolly, tilt and crane moves in portrait, depth of field, rack focus; short dramatic pushes for impact.
- Particles: dust, petals, fireflies, sparks. FX: speed lines, sparkle, impact frames used sparingly.
- Colour grade: filmic, soft contrast, palette locked per scene.
- Captions: 1 to 2 words, large, kept inside the portrait safe area (bottom third, above platform UI).
- Pacing: the upstream motion targets (docs/07) are for fast shorts. For this look, aim for continuous motion but allow slower
  cinematic beats; re-tune `tools/study/qa_motion.py` thresholds before using it as a hard gate.
